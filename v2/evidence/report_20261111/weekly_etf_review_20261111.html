How this portfolio works

  1. The portfolio has a core (VWCE for equities, EUNA for bonds), 5 satellites of about 8% each, and a small cash position (target 3%).
  2. Every week all funds in the universe are ranked on their 3-, 6- and 12-month returns. The strongest funds in an uptrend fill the satellite slots; one fund per theme or region.
  3. A held fund stays while it ranks in the top 15 and its trend is up. A newcomer replaces a holding only if it scores at least 25 points higher. A fund is sold on trend after two consecutive weeks below its 200-day average.
  4. When world equity is in a downtrend and most funds are too, the portfolio turns defensive: 3 satellites, with the freed-up weight in a money-market fund (XEON).
  5. At most 2 fund changes per week; excess cash is invested in steps.

Data and method

Prices: closing prices on Xetra and Euronext Amsterdam in euro (Yahoo Finance), with holdings checked against a second source (Alpha Vantage). Returns are price returns; for distributing funds (Dist) dividends are not included. Trades are modelled at the Wednesday close in whole shares with an estimated 0.10% cost per trade. Macro data: ECB Data Portal, Federal Reserve Bank of New York and Eurostat. The commentary is drafted with the help of AI from the figures in this report only; every number in the text is checked automatically against those figures.
