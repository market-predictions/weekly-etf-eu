How this portfolio works

  1. Core: VWCE (equities) and EUNA (bonds). Around it 5 satellites of about 8% and a small cash position (target 3%).
  2. Every week all funds are ranked on 3-, 6- and 12-month returns; the strongest in an uptrend fill the satellite slots, one per theme or region.
  3. A satellite stays while it ranks in the top 15 and its trend is up; a newcomer replaces it only if it scores at least 25 points higher. A trend sale follows two weeks below the 200-day average.
  4. Defensive when world equity is in a downtrend and breadth is weak: 3 satellites, the rest in a money-market fund (XEON). With inflation above 3% and rising, the core holds short-dated government bonds (IBGS).
  5. At most 2 fund changes per week. Aim: keep up with the market and limit large losses; the rules do not promise to beat the benchmark.

Data and method

Benchmark: 70% world equity (VWCE) and 30% global bonds (EUNA), rebalanced weekly. Prices: closing prices on Xetra and Euronext Amsterdam in euro (Yahoo Finance), with holdings checked against a second source (Alpha Vantage). Returns are price returns; for distributing funds (Dist) dividends are not included. Trades are modelled at the Wednesday close in whole shares with an estimated 0.10% cost per trade. Macro data: ECB Data Portal, Federal Reserve Bank of New York and Eurostat. The commentary is drafted with the help of AI from the figures in this report only; every number in the text is checked automatically against those figures.
